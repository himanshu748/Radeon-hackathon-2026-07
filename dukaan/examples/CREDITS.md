# Demo photographs

Dukaan's own code is Apache-2.0. The photographs used to demonstrate it are not
mine, so they are listed here with their source and licence.

All four are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), which is why
they were chosen: the creatives derived from them carry no further conditions.

| File | Object | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `brass-ewer.png` | Brass ewer, India, 18th-19th c. | Cleveland Museum of Art, accession 1922.320 | CC0 1.0 |
| `silver-bracelet.png` | Silver bracelet, India | Cleveland Museum of Art, accession 1916.231 | CC0 1.0 |
| `gilt-bangles.png` | Pair of gilt bronze bangles, India | Cleveland Museum of Art, accession 1973.64 | CC0 1.0 |
| `pot.png` | Synthetic test shape | Generated by `tests/`, not a photograph | Apache-2.0 |

Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access: https://openaccess-api.clevelandart.org/

They stand in for what a small seller would actually shoot: one object, one
plain surface, one phone. Museum object photography has the same shape as a
good counter photo, and unlike stock product shots it is unambiguously free.
